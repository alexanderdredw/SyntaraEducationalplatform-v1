A person can live for several weeks without food and several days without water, but only a few minutes without air. The respiratory system supplies the body with oxygen without a break.

## Why We Breathe {#functions}

Cells constantly need energy. They get it by breaking down nutrients with the help of oxygen. This produces carbon dioxide, which has to be removed from the body.

The respiratory system:

- delivers oxygen from the air into the blood;
- removes carbon dioxide from the blood;
- cleans, warms and moistens the air we breathe in;
- helps us produce our voice.

## Airways {#airways}

Air reaches the lungs through a system of tubes — the airways.

- **Nasal cavity.** Here air is warmed, moistened and cleaned: hairs and mucus trap dust and germs.
- **Larynx.** It holds the vocal cords. When we swallow, the **epiglottis** closes the entrance to the larynx, so food does not enter the airways.
- **Trachea** — a tube with rings of cartilage that keep it from collapsing.
- **Bronchi.** The trachea divides into two bronchi, which branch into thinner and thinner bronchioles, like the branches of a tree.

## Lungs and Alveoli {#lungs}

![Diagram of the end of a bronchiole: a cluster of alveoli wrapped in a network of capillaries](media/alveoli.png)

The lungs are a pair of organs in the chest. The right lung has three lobes and the left lung has two, because the heart sits next to it. A thin membrane, the pleura, covers the lungs.

The bronchioles end in tiny air sacs called **alveoli**. The wall of an alveolus is a single layer of cells, densely wrapped in capillaries. Oxygen from the air passes through the wall into the blood, and carbon dioxide passes from the blood into the alveolus. This process is called **gas exchange**.

## Breathing In and Out {#breathing}

The lungs have no muscles of their own. They are stretched and squeezed by the breathing muscles — the **diaphragm** and the intercostal muscles between the ribs.

- **Breathing in:** the diaphragm contracts and moves down, and the intercostal muscles lift the ribs. The chest gets bigger, the lungs expand and air flows in.
- **Breathing out:** the muscles relax, the chest gets smaller and air flows out.

At rest an adult takes about 16–18 breaths a minute. When running or swimming, breathing becomes faster and deeper.

## Healthy Breathing {#hygiene}

- Breathe through your nose: the air is cleaned and warmed better that way.
- Air your room regularly and spend more time outdoors.
- Do sports — they train your breathing muscles.
- Do not smoke and keep away from tobacco smoke: it damages the alveoli and bronchi.
- Cover your mouth and nose when you cough or sneeze so you do not infect others.
